# Innovation-gap taxonomy — scoped research hypotheses, not estimated causes

The 12 entries in the derived CSV translate project v3 A-WP4 example sectors into explicit research questions with a candidate model parameter, controlled test and known boundary. General topic references to IPCC AR6 WGIII chapters explain the relevance of each subject, but do not quantify any individual proposed intervention, validate local technology readiness or prove which IAM reports a parameter.

Every entry remains REFERENCE_TOPIC_ONLY_REQUIRES_CASE_REVIEW and UNTESTED_HYPOTHESIS. Ranking has deliberately not been assigned before an independent literature inclusion/exclusion and evidence-quality assessment. The register does not state relative causes of gross emissions or forecast climate outcomes. A later controlled experiment should prespecify identical assumptions and source-model parameters; observational differences among IAM families are not attribution.

Inputs: IIASA Real Zero independent v3 plan, sections A-WP4 and A.5, and IPCC WGIII Chapters 4, 6, 7, 10, 11, 12. No actual stakeholder prioritization, expert elicitation, or real-world deployment result is claimed.
