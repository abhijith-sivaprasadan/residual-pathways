## Explicit exclusions (still not automatically complete)

- External verification of Strefler CEMICS physical gross/removal boundaries, full 24-case manual accounting, source authorship clarification and I3 promotion.
- PathwaysHub authenticated real external human exchange/consent, which cannot be replaced by simulated actors.
- Genuine run-independent MESSAGEix reproduction by another researcher, and model calibration/empirical validation.
- Full scenario M2–M6 technology-growth/storage interaction matrix and complete CH4/F-gas/GHG CO2e harmonization: optional extensions beyond the core reproducible coverage report.
