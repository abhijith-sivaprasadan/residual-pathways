## Allowed claims

- Built and tested reproducible IAMC input checks, multi-year variable-reporting coverage, source-hash manifests, decadal signed CO2 reporting analysis and quantified selection-completeness contrasts on a teaching source.

- Built and tested an **author-only synthetic** research-intake, issue-correction and status-board workflow.

## Disallowed claims without new evidence

- Reconstructed published residual positive gross emissions or independently established I3 scenario classifications.
- Verified that teaching-source metrics equal all canonical AR6 World v1.1 source fields or that original metadata provenance matches.
- Executed MESSAGEix Westeros with GAMS, worked on MESSAGEix-GLOBIOM, or coordinated IIASA teams.
